Got Questions About Plugging?

How do I get paid?

Payments are held in vault escrow before you commence work. Upon delivery and student signoff, funds disburse instantly to your linked Nigerian bank or fintech wallet (OPay, PalmPay, Kuda).

What if a client cancels?

If you have commenced verified work and the client attempts sudden cancellation, our automated dispute mechanism reviews chat proof and releases partial or full compensation.

Can I offer multiple skills?

Yes! Once your primary onboarding is completed and student status is verified, you can add sub-services (e.g. photography + photo editing or hair braiding + wig revamping) inside your Plug Dashboard.
